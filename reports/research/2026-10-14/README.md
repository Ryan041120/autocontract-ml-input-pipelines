# AutoContract public evidence snapshot — 2026-10-14

[claims.json](claims.json) extracts specific findings from saved research records, with original record hashes and limitations. [model_examples.json](model_examples.json) contains three Normalize／Flip model contexts and one tiny owned Normalize／CenterCrop／stack context, each paired with its saved certificate.

These are public projections of known development records. They are not independent labels, new native experiment results, or a complete cross-method benchmark. Original contexts preserve their declared bindings; the offline check verifies the packaged pure models, not every dependency named in those contexts.

No original frozen record is altered. The original local record hashes support traceability; reproducing a projection does not establish independence or validate omitted native/runtime assumptions.
